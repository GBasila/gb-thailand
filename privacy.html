Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how GB MANAGEMENT (THAILAND) CO., LTD. (GB Management Thailand, "we") handles personal data when you contact us, including through WhatsApp at +66 63 228 7050. We follow Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What we collect

  • Your name and WhatsApp phone number, as shared by WhatsApp when you message us.
  • The messages, photos, links and files you choose to send us (for example, photos of your property).
  • Basic details about your property or business that you tell us.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss our services.
  • To prepare what you ask for, such as a promo video or a website proposal.
  • To keep a record of our conversation so we can follow up on your request.

We only message you after you contact us first, and we stop if you ask us to.

Who processes it

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) delivers messages between you and us.
  • AI assistance: we use an AI service (Anthropic) to help draft replies. A person from our team reviews and approves replies before they are sent.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising profiles.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation records for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires longer.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop contacting you. Send the word DELETE or your request to us on WhatsApp at +66 63 228 7050, and we will act on it within 30 days.
