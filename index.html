FAQ

Questions owners ask

Do I have to leave Airbnb or Booking.com?

No. Keep your listings. We add a direct channel next to them, so more guests can book with you directly.

What do I need to send?

Five to ten good photos of your villa or business, or a link to your current listing. We take it from there.

Is the first reel really free?

Yes. Send us your photos on WhatsApp and we make your first reel, so you can see the quality before you decide.

How much is the subscription?

One price for everyone: ฿990 a month, whether you have one villa, ten villas or a restaurant. It includes WhatsApp replies, your website, calendar sync, comment replies and 4 reels a month. Extra reels are ฿99 each, 10 for ฿790, or a reel every day for ฿1,990 a month — see Pricing. Your first reel is free, then a 7-day free trial.

Do you work with properties outside Thailand?

Yes. We are based on Koh Samui, but everything runs online — reels, your website, WhatsApp replies and calendar sync — so we work with villas, apartments and businesses anywhere in the world, for the same ฿990 a month.

Do you rent out or sell villas yourselves?

No. GB Management Thailand is a marketing and automation service. Every booking or sale is made directly between the owner and the guest or buyer. The owner is responsible for the booking, the contract, payments, licences and compliance with Thai law.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for speed: drafting scripts, voiceovers and first replies. A real person checks everything before it goes out.
